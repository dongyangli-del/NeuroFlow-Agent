# Claim Grounding

## Claim Audit

1. Write the exact claim.
2. Identify the closest prior work.
3. Separate method novelty, empirical gain, dataset contribution, and conceptual framing.
4. Map each claim to evidence or citation.
5. Mark unsupported parts.
6. Rewrite the claim with scoped wording.

## Reviewer Risk Signals

- Broad claims such as thought understanding, AGI, general intelligence, or brain reading.
- Claims of robustness without cross-subject, cross-session, or cross-dataset evidence.
- Reconstruction claims supported only by qualitative examples.
- Closed-loop claims based only on offline replay.
- Benchmark claims without fair baseline parity.
