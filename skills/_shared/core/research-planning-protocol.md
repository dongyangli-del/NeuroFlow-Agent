# Research Planning Protocol

Use this before deep or persistent NeuroFlow tasks, especially paper planning, benchmark selection, reproduction, and experiment design.

```markdown
Research question:
Why this workflow:
Primary claim or decision:
Expected evidence:
Assumptions:
Known constraints:
Failure modes:
Decision gate:
Verification target:
```

## Rules

- State the research question before selecting datasets, baselines, experiments, or writing strategy.
- Name why the selected workflow is appropriate instead of defaulting to a familiar method.
- Separate expected evidence from already inspected evidence.
- Treat unverified parameters as blockers for strong claims.
- End with a decision gate: what evidence would make the next step valid, invalid, or inconclusive.
